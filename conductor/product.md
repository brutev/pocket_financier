
# Pocket Financier Product Guide

## 1. Initial Concept
A Flutter-based personal finance management app that automatically extracts and categorizes banking transactions from SMS messages.

## 2. Product Vision
To empower users with effortless financial tracking and insights by leveraging automated SMS transaction parsing. The app aims to provide a comprehensive, secure, and user-friendly platform for managing personal finances, offering clear visualizations, smart analytics, and personalized financial coaching.

## 3. Core Features
- **SMS Transaction Parsing:** Automatically reads and processes banking SMS messages from major Indian banks, with advanced spam detection and high-accuracy extraction.
- **Transaction Management:** Provides automatic categorization of transactions (Food, Shopping, Bills, Rent, Fuel, etc.), credit/debit tracking, and local SQLite storage with real-time balance calculations.
- **Financial Dashboard:** Offers visual spending charts, monthly spending analysis, category-wise expense breakdown, and net savings tracking using FL Chart.
- **AI Financial Coach:** Delivers personalized financial advice, spending pattern analysis, and budget recommendations through integrated AI services (e.g., Flutter Gemma).

## 4. Target Audience
Individuals seeking an automated and intuitive solution for personal finance management, particularly those in India who receive banking transaction alerts via SMS. Users who prefer on-device data privacy and offline functionality.

## 5. Key Differentiators
- **Automated SMS Parsing:** Unique selling proposition for hands-free transaction tracking.
- **Offline First & Privacy-Focused:** All data processed and stored locally, ensuring user privacy and security.
- **AI-Powered Insights:** Offers intelligent financial coaching and recommendations.
- **Comprehensive Visualizations:** Provides clear and actionable financial insights through interactive charts.

## 6. Future Roadmap Highlights
- **Budget Management:** Ability to set and track budgets per category with alerts.
- **Recurring Transactions Detection:** Automatic identification and prediction of recurring expenses.
- **Data Export & Backup:** Options to export transactions and backup data (CSV/Excel, PDF, Cloud/Local).
- **Enhanced Analytics:** Deeper insights with year-over-year comparisons, spending trends, and merchant analysis.
- **Goals & Savings Tracker:** Tools to set and monitor savings goals.
- **UI/UX Enhancements:** Dark mode, advanced search and filtering, bill reminders, multi-currency support, transaction tags, and home screen widgets.
