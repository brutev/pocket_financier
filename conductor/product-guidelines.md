# Product Guidelines

## 1. Tone and Voice
The application will maintain a **Professional & Authoritative** tone. All communication, from UI text to notifications, should be clear, concise, and credible. This voice builds user trust by emphasizing data accuracy and the security of their financial information.

## 2. Visual Identity
The visual style will be **Modern & Dynamic**. The UI will incorporate contemporary design trends, utilizing a vibrant color palette and interactive elements to create an engaging user experience. While being dynamic, the design must prioritize data clarity and readability, particularly in charts and financial reports.

## 3. Communication Style
The AI Financial Coach will adopt an **Insightful & Explanatory** communication style. Recommendations and advice should be accompanied by detailed explanations that help users understand the reasoning behind them. The goal is to educate and empower users, not just to instruct.

## 4. Prose Style
All written content should be direct, data-driven, and objective. Avoid overly casual language, slang, or emojis. The prose should reflect the professional tone of the application, ensuring that users feel they are interacting with a reliable and expert financial tool.

## 5. Visual Identity & Brand Messaging
- **Brand Messaging:** The core message is "Effortless financial clarity." The app empowers users by making complex financial data easy to understand and act upon.
- **Color Palette:** The primary color palette should be based on the existing `fl_chart` implementation, with an extended palette for "Modern & Dynamic" elements.
- **Typography:** Fonts must be clean, legible, and professional.

## 6. Content Strategy
The content strategy will focus on delivering value through data-driven insights. Key content areas include:
- **Transaction Summaries:** Clear, automated summaries of spending and income.
- **Analytical Reports:** In-depth reports on spending habits, trends, and comparisons.
- **Educational Insights:** Explanations of financial concepts related to the user's data.
